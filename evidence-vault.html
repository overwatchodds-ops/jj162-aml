Document Register
& Recordkeeping

Keep AML/CTF records clearly organised, stored locally in your browser on your device, and regularly exported for backup and retention.

The Requirement

What records do you need to keep?

Under the AML/CTF Act, it is not enough to complete checks. You also need to keep a clear record that those checks were completed, what the result was, and when the record was created or reviewed.

For most accounting practices, that means keeping a record of:

  • Client identification and verification steps completed
  • PEP and sanctions screening dates and results
  • Client risk ratings and review dates
  • Beneficial ownership checks completed
  • Suspicious matter and incident review records
  • Training, personnel due diligence, and AML/CTF program approval records

7-year retention requirement. AML/CTF records generally need to be retained for at least 7 years. Your firm remains responsible for meeting those retention obligations.

How SimpleAML Works

What SimpleAML does — and what it does not do

SimpleAML is a compliance register. It helps you keep a clear record of what checks were completed, who completed them, when they were done, and where supporting evidence is kept.

SimpleAML stores records locally in your browser on your device. It does not currently provide multi-user cloud sync. You should export and back up your records regularly.

SimpleAML is not a hosted document repository. It does not replace your firm’s own filing system for identity documents, screening reports, AML/CTF program files, or other source materials.

Plain-English Summary

Who this is best for

Best for sole practitioners and small firms. SimpleAML is most suitable for sole practitioners and smaller firms where one person mainly manages AML/CTF compliance.

Browser-based local storage. Data is stored in the browser on your device, not in a shared cloud account run by SimpleAML.

Regular export and backups required. You should export and save backups regularly because browser data may be lost if your device, browser, or settings change.

Multi-user cloud sync not currently available. SimpleAML does not currently support shared cloud syncing across multiple users or devices.

What the App Helps You Record

What the register can help you track

Recordkeeping support

  • Client AML/CTF record trail, including risk rating and review status
  • Dates and outcomes of PEP, sanctions, and identity checks
  • Training and personnel due diligence recordkeeping
  • Incident and SMR review records
  • Program approval, review dates, and compliance summary information
  • Regular export for backup and record retention purposes

SimpleAML is designed to help smaller firms stay organised and audit-ready without introducing a separate hosted storage system.
